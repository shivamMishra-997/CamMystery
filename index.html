About the studio

Quietly present. Beautifully timed.

Cam Mystery is a wedding photography studio built on one belief: the best photographs are never staged. We blend into your celebration, read the room and wait for the moments that make your wedding yours.

From intimate ceremonies to week-long celebrations, we shape every project around your rituals, your families and your story, and deliver it with the care of a handmade album.

Sajib Bhowmik, founder
