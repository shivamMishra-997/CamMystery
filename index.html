About us

Capturing timeless Indian wedding stories

Welcome to Shaadi Seasons, where sacred rituals, vibrant Indian heritage and deep emotions meet timeless visual storytelling.

Our story & craft

We are a premier Indian wedding photography studio dedicated to capturing the soul, grandeur and spontaneous magic of South Asian weddings. From the quiet, intricate moments of the Mehendi to the grand energy of the Baraat and the timeless promises around the holy Pheras, we craft cinematic heirlooms.

Our studio was founded on a simple belief: your wedding photos should feel as vivid, honest and magical as the moments themselves.

We combine the artistic eye of candid photojournalism with the polished elegance of high-fashion editorial portraiture. Every image honours the rich textures of traditional craftsmanship, from the golden glow of marigold mandaps to the details of bridal lehengas and royal heritage aesthetics.

Manish Susodhiya, founder
